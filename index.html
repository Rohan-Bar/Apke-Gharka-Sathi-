Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, technician safety, and pricing.

How do you ensure the safety and skill of technicians?

Every service partner undergoes a rigorous multi-stage KYC verification process, in-person skill assessment, and police background clearance before being onboarded to the platform.

Are prices fixed, or can the technician charge extra?

All rates are strictly governed by our standardized rate card displayed in the app. If any additional spare parts are needed, they are itemized in your app before work begins for complete transparency.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay securely using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, or choose Cash on Delivery (COD) once you are completely satisfied with the repair.

What if the issue recurs after the technician leaves?

All bookings come with our Apke Gharka Sathi 30-Day Service Guarantee. If the repaired problem recurs within 30 days, we send a technician for a free re-visit and resolution.
